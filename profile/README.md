## Google review removal for businesses

**An unfair Google review can cost you trust and customers. Start with a free eligibility check.**

Reviewell helps businesses challenge fake, abusive or potentially policy-breaking Google reviews: assess the case, organise the evidence and pursue an appropriate removal route.

[**Get a free Google review check →**](https://www.reviewell.com/#check) · [Google review removal](https://www.reviewell.com/google-review-removal) · [Pricing](https://www.reviewell.com/pricing)

### What we help with

- **Suspected fake reviews** and reviews from people whose experience is disputed.
- **Review attacks**, including possible competitor conflicts or coordinated activity.
- **Abusive or inappropriate content** that may breach platform rules.
- **Case preparation and follow-up**, with relevant evidence and clear next steps.

### From review to next step

**1. Share the review.** Send the link and explain what happened.<br />
**2. Get the case assessed.** Reviewell checks the context and identifies the relevant evidence and route.<br />
**3. Agree the next action.** Move forward with a clear scope and follow the case through the client workspace.

The review platform decides whether content is removed. Genuine criticism is not automatically eligible for removal.
