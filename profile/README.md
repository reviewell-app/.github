## Content removal & reputation protection

Reviewell is building a casework platform for disputed reviews, fake profiles, exposed personal data and other harmful online content. The work starts with the facts and an assessment of the appropriate route.

**Status: public website preview. The client workspace requires an account.**

[**Explore the public preview →**](https://reviewell-removal-preview.vercel.app) · [Take the guided tour](https://github.com/reviewell-app/.github/blob/main/docs/DEMO.md) · [Official website](https://reviewell.com)

### Explore three public views

**01 · Identify the issue**<br />
Open the [intake preview](https://reviewell-removal-preview.vercel.app/#check) to see how a review, profile, image or privacy concern enters the workflow.

**02 · Understand the evidence**<br />
The [evidence guide](https://reviewell-removal-preview.vercel.app/evidence-guide) explains what information helps a case assessment.

**03 · Follow the process**<br />
See [how the process works](https://reviewell-removal-preview.vercel.app/how-it-works), from context and assessment to preparation and follow-up.

The [guided tour](https://github.com/reviewell-app/.github/blob/main/docs/DEMO.md) uses fictional context and requires no account or case submission.

### Who it is for

**Businesses and individuals:** understand the next step and keep relevant context together.<br />
**Agencies and caseworkers:** coordinate evidence requests, decisions and case history.

A platform decides its own outcomes; assessment does not guarantee removal.
