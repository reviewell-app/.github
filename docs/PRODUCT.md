# Reviewell — service and product overview

[Start a free Google review check](https://www.reviewell.com/#check) · [Google review removal](https://www.reviewell.com/google-review-removal) · [Pricing](https://www.reviewell.com/pricing)

## Main commercial service

Reviewell helps businesses assess and pursue removal requests for Google reviews that may breach platform rules. The starting point is a free eligibility check: the review URL, the business's explanation and relevant context.

The service is relevant to suspected fake reviews, possible conflicts of interest, coordinated attacks and abusive or inappropriate content. Each case needs its own factual assessment. A removal outcome is decided by the review platform and cannot be guaranteed.

## The customer journey

1. **Free check:** share the review and describe the concern.
2. **Assessment:** establish the facts, identify the possible route and request relevant evidence.
3. **Agreed scope:** understand what work is proposed and the applicable commercial terms.
4. **Casework:** prepare the record and follow the agreed next steps.
5. **Follow-up:** keep the client informed and record the platform response or other outcome.

Current service options and prices are maintained on the [pricing page](https://www.reviewell.com/pricing).
