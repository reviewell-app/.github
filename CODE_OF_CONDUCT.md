# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful, useful and focused on the work. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Give specific feedback about ideas and code, and respect people's time and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, spam and publishing private information are not acceptable.

For a private conduct report, use GitHub's **Report abuse** feature on the relevant content. Do not repeat private or abusive material in a public issue. Repository maintainers may moderate content and restrict participation to protect the community.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project community spaces.
